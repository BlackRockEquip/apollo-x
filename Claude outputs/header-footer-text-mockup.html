How it would work

Both texts are optional; an empty box prints nothing.

  • Header sits at the very top of the page, above the logo and company details, on every page.
  • Footer sits at the bottom of each page, above the page number and "Powered by" line.
  • Applies to delivery notes, job cards, pick slips, parts lists, reports and saved PDFs.
  • Line breaks you type are kept. Suggested use: tagline or notice on top; bank details and terms at the bottom.
