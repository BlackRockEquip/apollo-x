What it does

The whole job, from the first call to the delivery note.

Each part of the business is its own module. They share the same customers, parts and jobs, so nothing is typed twice.

Jobs & WIP

Job cards, status steps, notes, attachments and a full history. Print the job card, delivery note and pick slip as clean PDFs.

Parts & ordering

Build a parts list on every job, see what is outstanding, apply reusable job kits and follow up with suppliers.

RFQs & supplier quotes

Send requests for quotation by email, import supplier replies and compare quotes side by side.

Stock & pick slips

Stock levels by bin and location, stock counts, reservations and pick slips that issue parts from the shelf to a job.

PEX exchange tracking

Supply and return jobs, units on the shelf, quarantine and scrap — with the full history of every unit.

Reports & ratios

Jobs per customer, warranty, monthly trends, on-time delivery, turnaround and repeat-customer rates.

Outwork

Send components to other shops, record when they leave and return, and keep the paperwork on the job.

Field service

Plan who is going where, record what was done on site and print the field report.

Specifications

For engineering businesses: dimensional size reports recorded on arrival and after machining, with the original handwritten sheet kept on file.

Industry builds

Starts with the right modules for your business.

Choose a build when your company is set up. You can switch individual modules on or off later as the business changes.

Workshop Business

Repair, rebuild and field service workshops

For workshops that quote parts, issue pick slips and track components through the shop.

  • Jobs, components, delivery notes and mechanics

Engineering Business

Engineering and machining shops

  • Stock and outwork
  • Reports on turnaround and delivery
How it works

Up and running without a long project.

We set up your company

Your company is created with its own branding, logo, numbering and the modules that fit your business.

Add your people and data

Invite users with the right role, and import customers, suppliers and parts from Excel or CSV.

Run your jobs

Create jobs, order parts, issue stock and keep customers informed. Switch on more modules whenever you are ready.

Roles and permissions

Administrators, managers, mechanics, store controllers and sales each see what they need. Modules can also be switched off per user.

Your branding

Your logo and colours appear in the system and on printed documents such as job cards and delivery notes.

Import and export

Bring customers, suppliers, jobs and parts in from spreadsheets, and export your data back out when you need it.

Security

Your data stays yours.

Flo X serves many companies from one platform, so keeping each company's information apart is built into every part of the system.

Separate by company

Every record belongs to one company and cannot be seen from another.

Protected sign-in

Passwords are stored securely, repeated failed sign-ins are locked out, and password reset links expire.

Audit trail

Important actions are recorded with who did them and when, so you can see what changed.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I have to use every module?

No. Each part of Flo X is a separate module. Your company starts with the modules for its build and you can switch others on or off as your needs change.

Can different staff see different things?

Yes. Roles control what each person can do, and individual modules can be switched off for a single user.

Can I bring my existing customers, suppliers and parts?

Yes. Customers, suppliers, jobs and parts can be imported from Excel or CSV files, and exported again at any time.

Will it show my company's name and logo?

Yes. Your logo and colours are used throughout the system and on printed documents like job cards, delivery notes and pick slips.

Can I try it before talking to anyone?

Yes. Open the live demo to explore a sample workshop with real-looking jobs, parts and stock. You can switch between roles such as admin, manager and mechanic. Nothing you do there is saved.

Where do I log in?

Use the Log in button at the top of this page. If you do not have an account yet, ask your company administrator, or contact us below.

How is pricing worked out?

Pricing depends on the modules your company uses. Get in touch and we will put a proposal together.
