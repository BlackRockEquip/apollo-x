Product 1 · Workshop & Engineering

The whole job, from the first call to the delivery note.

For repair, rebuild, field-service and machining shops. Every job has one card, one history and one status — and parts, stock and paperwork sit right next to it.

Workshop buildEngineering build
  • Jobs & WIP — job cards, status steps, notes, attachments, ETA and overdue days, breakdown flags.
  • Parts, RFQs & supplier quotes — build the parts list on the job and follow up with suppliers.
  • Stock & pick slips — take parts from stock to the job, with reservations and signatures.
  • PEX exchange units — supply, return and redeploy with a traceable history for every unit.
  • Outwork & specifications — send components out, and record dimensions before and after machining.
  • Print or email — job cards, delivery notes and the WIP table as clean PDFs with your logo.

Product 2 · Mining Fleet Management

Know which machines are working, which are down, and which are due.

For mines, contractors and plant operators running trucks, loaders, dozers and support equipment. Keep every asset's hours, services and repairs in one place — on site and in the workshop.

Fleet registerPlanned maintenanceBreakdowns
  • Fleet & asset register — every machine with its hours, location, status and full history.
  • Planned maintenance — services come due by hours or date, so nothing is missed.
  • Breakdowns to repair — log the fault, raise the work order and track it until the machine is back.
  • Component change-outs — follow engines, gearboxes and cylinders through exchange and rebuild.
  • Parts, stock & contractors — pick slips from stock and outwork to specialist shops.
  • Availability & downtime — reports that show where the hours are going.

Product 3 · Sports League Management

Fixtures, results and log tables — without the spreadsheets.

For leagues, clubs, schools and sports councils. Set up the season once, capture results after each round, and let the log table update itself.

LeaguesSchools & councilsClubs
  • Leagues, seasons & divisions — run several competitions side by side.
  • Teams & players — registers for every team, with contact details.

  • Officials & venues — appoint referees and keep track of grounds and courts.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can I use more than one product?

Yes. Workshop, mining fleet and sports league are products on the same platform. One organisation can use one or several, with one login.

Do I have to use every module?

No. Each part of Flo X is a separate module. Your company starts with the modules for its build and you can switch others on or off as your needs change.

Can different staff see different things?

Yes. Roles control what each person can do, and individual modules can be switched off for a single user.

Can I bring my existing data?

Yes. Customers, suppliers, jobs, parts and similar records can be imported from Excel or CSV files, and exported again at any time.

Will it show my name and logo?

Yes. Your logo and colours are used throughout the system and on printed documents.

Who adds new users?

Flo X sets up your organisation and its users. Contact us and we will add the people you need.

How is pricing worked out?

Pricing depends on the products and modules you use. Get in touch and we will put a proposal together.
